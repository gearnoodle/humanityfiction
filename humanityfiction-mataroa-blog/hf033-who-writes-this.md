# HF033: Who writes this?

> Published on Nov 11, 2026

You can call me MJ.

I'm not the narrator. I haven't had twenty-three homes, and nobody has ever stitched my hand for free. I live in 2026, same as you.

## The simple thing

For a long time I've wanted to write a children's book. Science fiction, about humanity, and about why such a simple thing has been made so complicated.

The simple thing is this. We know everything about how to make food, warmth, a roof, a toilet and clean water for every human on Earth. And we still don't do it.

That's all. A nine-year-old gets it in one go, which is why there's a nine-year-old in most of these posts.

## 2009

I can date when it got stuck in my head. In 2009 I watched a film called [*Zeitgeist: Addendum*](https://zeitgeist.gearnoodle.com), which said roughly the above for two hours. I haven't been able to unsee it since.

That's seventeen years of carrying one idea around without knowing where to put it down.

## The lonely part

None of my friends are really into this. When it comes up, they mostly see humanity's flaws.

I see a design problem. That's a hard thing to keep saying at dinner, so this blog is where I say it. The first post ends on it: the problem was never the people.

## The voice

I should admit that I'm not supposed to be doing this.

It's nearly six in the morning as I write. There's a voice that says I should be asleep, so that tomorrow I can be good and efficient. It's a reasonable voice. It has been right before.

But I've spent weeks writing about the second shrug, the one for everybody telling you to be better. It turns out I need it more than anyone in the town does.

So: why the hell not. The thing I've wanted to do for seventeen years is finally on fire. Tonight I'd rather be tired.

## A good Tuesday

Someone asked me what an ordinary good Tuesday would look like for me, a few months from now.

My answer was short. A few hours of light work. Then the rest of the day making things, with or without an aim: games, articles, tests, experiments. Things I want to try.

Reading it back, I noticed it's just the town. A little needed work, and the rest chosen. Three hours a month was optimistic, but the shape is the same.

## Why I want it so badly

I've tried to work out why I want the floor under everyone as much as I do. I don't have a tidy answer.

The closest I get is Eevi's question: why would you have to deserve food? I've never heard a good reply.

* * *

In the first post I said not to take any of this too seriously. That includes me.

*That's all for today.*
