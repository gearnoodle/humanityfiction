# WM002: Take it easy

> Published on Nov 20, 2026

On HF002. A man opens the Earth-monitor: a pump to inspect, a beginner to teach, too many courgettes, a cat in a weather station, a bridge getting worse. He's tired. He takes it easy. [HF002]

**Balthazar:** "It knows where I live, what I understand, what catches my curiosity." That's a profile. Every app promised it wasn't using that against you.

**Caspar:** Every app sold adverts. Nobody here sells anything. And your page stays on your own device. [HF000]

**Balthazar:** Until someone wants power instead of money.

**Melchior:** Fair. Go on.

**Balthazar:** "It does not command." It shows you a bridge falling down. That *is* a command. Guilt doesn't need a boss.

**Caspar:** The old news showed you wars you couldn't stop. This shows you a pump you can fix.

**Balthazar:** News made me feel guilty about the world. This makes me feel guilty about my street.

**Caspar:** Your street, you can fix.

**Balthazar:** And the bridge? He's tired. Everyone's tired some days. Who goes?

**Caspar:** Whoever is on the list for bridges. If nobody is, it climbs everyone's page. If it's dangerous, it's an emergency. [HF000]

**Balthazar:** And the cat gets more volunteers than the bridge, because the feed shows "what catches my curiosity".

**Caspar:** Good. Curiosity is how people start. They come for the cat and stay for the weather station.

**Balthazar:** And everyone waking at 4:17? That's the monitor. They're checking it.

*Caspar laughed.*

**Melchior:** The best line is the decision. Pump, teaching, research, bridge, and he picks none. Nobody fines him. Nobody even notices.

**Balthazar:** He notices.

**Melchior:** Yes. "It simply makes ignorance less available." Once you know about the bridge, you can't un-know it. That's a weight.

**Caspar:** A light one, if the list has an end.

**Melchior:** Drills have a duty cycle. Run it so long, let it cool, or the motor burns out. Rest isn't the opposite of work. It's part of it.

**Balthazar:** Drills don't feel guilty.

**Melchior:** No. And that's the real risk. Not the lazy ones. The generous ones who never stop. [HF000]

**Balthazar:** So the lazy are fine and the good ones break.

**Melchior:** Same as now. Just with fewer excuses.

## Where they left it

**Balthazar:** A to-do list with no boss is still a to-do list. And it's watching you.

**Melchior:** Rest is part of the work. The danger isn't the lazy. It's the ones who can't stop.

**Caspar:** A man was tired, took the day off, and nothing broke. Best sentence in the blog.

Balthazar left first. Work, he said, already checking his phone.
